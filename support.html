Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email lukereminders@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

It helps to include your Mac’s macOS version and Luke’s version (Luke → About Luke). Please don’t send passwords or anything sensitive.

Please don’t rely on Luke for critical reminders like medication or deadlines you can’t miss. Reminders can be late or missed if your Mac is asleep, notifications are off, or Focus is on. More in our Terms of Use.

Using Luke

How do I add something quickly?

Click the floating bubble, or press ⌃⌘L (Control-Command-L) from any app to open the quick panel. Press ⌥⌘L (Option-Command-L) to open the full Luke window. You can change both shortcuts in Settings.

How do I set a date or a repeating reminder?

Use the date buttons, or type a date after @, for example Call mom @tomorrow 5pm or Send draft @fri. For repeats, type @daily 8am or @every monday 9am, or choose Repeat. Always check the date Luke shows before you rely on it.

I deleted something by mistake.

Right after deleting, click Undo. Later, look in Recently Deleted in the sidebar. Items stay there for 30 days, then they’re removed for good.

How do I see the tour again?

Open Settings and click Show the tour again.

Reminders and notifications

My reminders don’t appear.
  1. Open System Settings → Notifications → Luke and make sure Allow notifications is on. Choose Alerts if you want them to stay on screen until you act.
  2. Check that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn’t on, or allow Luke in your Focus settings.
  3. Make sure your Mac’s date, time and time zone are correct.
  4. Keep Luke open. Turn on Open Luke when your Mac starts in Luke’s Settings so it’s always ready.

Reminders can’t appear while your Mac is off or asleep. You’ll see missed ones as overdue in Luke.

Your data

Where are my tasks and notes saved?

Only on your Mac, in Luke’s own private folder. Luke has no account and doesn’t sync, so we can’t see or recover your data. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up, or move to another Mac?

In Luke, open Settings → Your Data → Export… and save the file somewhere safe. On the other Mac, open Settings → Your Data → Import… and pick that file. Import adds the items to what’s already there. The backup file isn’t encrypted, so treat it like any private document.

How do I delete all of my data?
  1. If you want a copy first, use Export.
  2. Quit Luke and drag it from Applications to the Trash.
  3. In Finder, choose Go → Go to Folder…, type ~/Library/Containers, and press Return.
  4. Drag the folder named Luke to the Trash, then empty the Trash.

Deleting Luke doesn’t cancel Luke Unlimited. See below.

Luke Unlimited

What’s free, and what does Luke Unlimited add?

The free plan includes up to 15 open notes and tasks without dates, and up to 10 open reminders, at a time. Checking off or deleting one frees its spot. Luke Unlimited removes both limits for US$0.99 a month or US$7.99 a year (prices vary by country), with a free 1-week trial for new subscribers.

How do I cancel?

In Luke, open Settings → Manage subscription. Or open the App Store, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then Account Settings → Subscriptions → Manage. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. Apple’s steps: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. We can’t see your payment or issue refunds ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account you used, and choose Luke.

I subscribed, but Luke still shows the free plan.

Open the upgrade screen or Settings and click Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you subscribed with. Luke Unlimited works on every Mac signed in to that account.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted or locked. Your tasks, notes and reminders keep working. You just can’t add new notes or tasks while you have 15 or more open ones, or new reminders while you have 10 or more.
