Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

In short: Luke doesn’t collect any personal data. Your tasks, notes and reminders are saved only on your Mac. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no Luke servers.

This policy explains how Luke, the Mac app made by Paola Sancho (“we”, “us”), handles information. It covers the app and this website.

What stays on your Mac

Everything you write in Luke (tasks, notes, reminders, their dates and repeats) and your settings are saved in Luke’s own private folder on your Mac. Luke never sends them anywhere. We can’t see them, and we can’t recover them for you.

If you use Export, Luke saves a backup file where you choose. That file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Import only reads a file you pick.

To protect what’s on your Mac, we recommend a login password and FileVault, which you can turn on in System Settings.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled with macOS on your Mac. A reminder’s text appears in its notification, and depending on your settings, may be visible on your screen or in Notification Center. You control this in System Settings → Notifications → Luke.

Purchases

Luke Unlimited is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. Luke asks the App Store on your Mac whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock Luke Unlimited. Apple gives us sales reports, such as the number of subscriptions by country, that don’t identify you. Apple handles purchase information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information Apple may share with us

If you choose in your Mac’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple may also show us anonymous App Store statistics, such as how many people viewed or downloaded Luke. We use this only to fix problems and improve Luke. None of it includes what you write in Luke.

Ratings and reviews you leave on the App Store are public and are handled by Apple.

If you email us

If you write to lukereminders@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use them only to reply and help you. We don’t sell them, share them, or add you to a mailing list. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, and we’ll delete them if you ask. Please don’t send us anything sensitive. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail).

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information, such as your IP address, to run and secure its service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell, rent or share personal information. We don’t have any.
  • We don’t track you across apps or websites.
  • We don’t show ads or include third-party advertising or analytics code in Luke.

Children

Luke is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

Because your information stays on your Mac, you are always in control of it. You can change or delete anything in Luke at any time. To remove everything, delete Luke and then its folder (shown as Luke) in your Library’s Containers folder. Steps are on the Support page.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to how it is used. Since we don’t collect personal information through Luke, we usually won’t have any to share. If you have a question or request, email us and we’ll answer.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If Luke ever starts handling your information differently, we’ll explain the change clearly in Luke and here before it happens.
